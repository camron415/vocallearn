## What they actually experience

### Day 1 — first invite (live today)

1. Paper login / join. Name. Land on empty Home. `Good evening, Mom.` Composer. Suggest chips: weather, news, dinner, sleep, watch… (desktop idle). Phone: tap focuses; no idle suggest.
2. She asks the weather or a capital. Stream. Sometimes spans light up and orbs fly to header dots. Sometimes nothing (correct skip). No sentence explains either case.
3. Header dots sit there. Tap does nothing. ◎ is empty. Library is recipes if she cooked.
4. Home is still empty of chips. She may think harvest was a glitch, or that the app is “chat with extra animation.”
5. She leaves. The unique loop is invisible. Wife/parents already flagged the wait — they had a demo. Uncoached, there is no wait to flag; there is nothing to wait **for**.

**Simple:** She used ChatGPT in a prettier room. She did not join Halo.

### Day 1 — if month-1 plan ships (storyboard)

1. Same empty Home. **No** fake Nile. **No** “Ask something worth remembering” (preachy; also fights Ask-first). Greeting stays the greeting.
2. She asks something closed and personal (“What’s the capital of Maine?” / a homework fact / a family-history date). Intent harvest keeps the answer, skips weather.
3. Spans + fly (frozen motion). **One line, one time, after land:** `Kept — these come back tomorrow.` If lock-in is live: line waits until skip or finish (see copy sheet).
4. She can tap a bead: prompt, answer, quiet rank word (`New`). Dismiss. Collection is real.
5. If encode-now is live: short try-once in chat (`Try these once` → SEE glance → say back). Skip is honorable: `Skip — keep for tomorrow.` Fly after. Home still empty this visit.
6. She may Ask again. Second harvest does **not** repeat the tour line.

**Proof of day 1:** she can say, in her own words, “it saved the answer up top and I’ll see it tomorrow.” If she cannot, onboarding failed even if harvest was correct.

### Day 2 — first due (live today)

Chips on Home (up to 16). `Good morning, Mom.` No “time to review.” Tap → round. End `You did good.` Day cap line if she keeps tapping. She only gets here if she opens the app.

**Simple:** The daily tidy is excellent **if she arrives**. Nothing fetches her.

### Day 2 — if month-1 plan ships

1. Open → greeting unchanged (not “You have 3 due!” — that is streak energy). Chips **are** the event. Cove vision: due day is the event.
2. Optional **first-due only** whisper under greeting, then never again: `These are from yesterday.` One clause. Not a tutorial.
3. Tap cluster → same Sunday round. Do not add “next review in 3 days” on the end card (spec freeze).
4. After Done: Home empties or remainder sits. `You're clear` only if she actually cleared **today’s** due (already the 1.1.1 rule).
5. Beads that passed show a metal rim in Keep. She can inspect. Gold still waits for r3.

**Proof of day 2:** she finishes one round without Camron. That is the family gate.

### Day 14 — habit without anxiety

Live today: if she kept opening, some facts are silver / heading to gold; ◎ may have moved; she still cannot inspect in-progress beads; she may have hit silent Keep 30; she may have harvested socks (pre-intent dump). If she forgot the app for 10 days, chips waited and she never knew.

Month-1 success looks like:

- She opened on her own **most** mornings there was something due — not because a flame would die, because Home had **her** questions sitting out.
- A few facts went gold. ◎ means “I kept that,” not a leaderboard.
- She still Asks when curious (weather stays legal; it just does not become a chip).
- Day cap told her to stop at least once. She did not feel punished.
- She has never seen Teach-me, a streak, a paywall, or an App Store wrapper.
- She can inspect Keep. Overflow, if she hit 30, is honest (`Keep is full`) not silent drop — Lane 3 owns the rule; we own the sentence.

**Simple:** Day 14 is a small private library of her curiosity plus a calm tidy. If we need a badge or a lesson button to make that true, the loop failed.

## One page — successful consumer learning product, month 1

Halo’s bet is **just-in-time cards from real questions** — Quizlet without a blank deck, Duolingo without a content factory, Anki without a power-user ritual, Readwise without a reading pipeline. The comparable products all made **one job** obviously work before they spread:

| Product | Month-1 job | Halo equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anki | Add a card, see it again on a schedule you trust | Harvest the asked fact; due tomorrow means tomorrow |
| Quizlet | Make / study a set in 30 seconds | Ask → see the fact in Keep → retrieve once |
| Readwise | Highlight in, review out | Question in, chip out, round out |
| Duolingo | A 5-minute lesson you understand | The Home round — **after** you know why it appeared |

None of them led with a second door (tutor), a store, or a native wrapper.

**North star (month 1):** three family members, uncoached, can: (1) ask something that matters to them, (2) recognize that Halo kept the answer, (3) the next day, tap a chip and finish a round, (4) say they might do that again. Fun = the round + beads they can inspect. Addictive = “I wonder if yesterday’s thing is waiting,” not a streak number.

**Week 1 — trust + name the loop.** Promote intent harvest when Lane 2/11 say skip/tangent are honest. Ship H1 copy. Do not demo Nile on real accounts. Camron may still stand over **once** to confirm harvest; that does not count as uncoached proof.

**Week 2 — collection + encoding.** Bead inspect (K1–K2). Encode-now with family copy (H2–H7), skip allowed, Home still empty same visit. Kill LearnReview leak. Optional A2 first-due whisper.

**Week 3 — proof.** Wife + parents (and a third) use live `/ask` without a walkthrough. Lane 11 watches whether they harvest, return, finish. Failure modes: they only Ask weather; they ignore Home chips; they think beads are decoration; they bounce at SAY typing on Safari (Lane 6). Fix the failing comprehension, not the roadmap.

**Week 4 — cost and freeze.** Luna if smoke is green. Overflow sentence if cap 30 bit anyone. Write what family said in one paragraph for chief. **Do not** start Teach-me, Learn-more, streaks, Stripe, iOS. Re-date 1.3–1.6 after this paragraph, not before.

**Metrics (human, not vanity)**

- Uncoached round completes: **3 people** (gate).
- After first harvest, can they paraphrase H1? (Camron asks once, then never prompts again.)
- Day-2 open without a text from Camron: at least **2 / 3**.
- Tangent / socks on live turns: falling (Lane 2).
- Do not track: streak length, XP, lessons started, waitlist size.

**If we only get one implementation ticket from this lane:** ship **H1** with harvest promote. It is the smallest thing that turns a flying orb into a product.
