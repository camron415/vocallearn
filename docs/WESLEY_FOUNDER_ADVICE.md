## The meeting (2026-09-12)

Wesley arrived a few minutes after 9:05. ~15–16 minutes. He opened immediately: **what are you building / what’s going on.**

**Camron’s rundown (paraphrase):** he builds software; people ask ChatGPT/Google and do not remember; no loop; they do not earn the knowledge; AI can make people passive; he built something that uses AI to teach on the questions they ask, records what they are learning, spaced repetition until they retain what they want to remember.

Pause. Camron offered to **show it**. Wesley yes. Safari on phone: WWII-style demo, looks like chat, then stores facts and you review. Honest: **early access, friends/family, not quite ready**; later same-visit review, etc. Showing a live thing vs a dream mattered.

**Wesley’s thread — “harnesses”:** models are smart; the unique product is the **niche harness** around the model (finance, lawyers, etc.), not another generic frontier chat. Lots of SaaS getting eaten by AI.

**Camron pushed back (good):** many tools take over work for efficiency; something that makes you **earn** learning, especially for students, is not the default trajectory of AI. Wesley then asked about **audience**, including **children** / personalized tutors if you can get **parent trust**. Camron: there is a market if parents trust it; he has looked at it.

**Start-over / how to proceed:** Wesley said five years ago he would have said get a team and build the app for the need. **Now:** stay **solo**, use AI as the team, do not look for partners/employees the way the physical-product person he talked to yesterday wanted to hire. Be active **where AI people are — X (Twitter)**. Keep building. Ask users what they like / which features they use. **Real test after more people: charge**, or ask how much they would pay and for what; if they will not pay, maybe it is an open-source tool with value, not a business.

**Close:** keep building; **you can always follow up / let him know how it goes / we can meet again** (no date). Keep the day job. No named intros, rooms, or “talk to this person.”

Camron did not dump Atlas, Kalshi, or the job search. He did not ask for money. He showed the product.
